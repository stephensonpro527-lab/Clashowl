Add your trademarks for deadline reminders

Already a ClashOwl subscriber? List the trademarks you've already filed or registered. We'll check the USPTO's daily files and email you before the dates that matter:

  • Office Action replies: when the USPTO raises a problem, your reply is usually due in 3 months (you can extend once). We remind you about 30, 14, and 3 days before.
  • Statement of Use: after a Notice of Allowance, you have 6 months to show use or file an extension.
  • Renewals: the Section 8 filing due between years 5 and 6, and the Section 8 & 9 renewal every 10 years.
  • Status changes: published for opposition, registered, or abandoned.

Where to find your number

Search your mark on USPTO Trademark Search or open it in TSDR. The serial number has 8 digits (for example 99755173). A registration number is usually 7 digits. Either one works.

Please read

These are reminders only. They are not legal advice and do not replace your own docket or an attorney. Dates are worked out from public USPTO data and can be off, especially for international (Madrid) filings, suspensions, or petitions. Always confirm the real deadline in TSDR. Older marks may not show anything until their next USPTO update.
