Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

ClashOwl is run by ClashOwl LLC, a California limited liability company. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it. If anything here is unclear, email us at support@clashowl.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address. When you start a trial, subscribe, sign up for our free tips, or use the free name check.
  • The brand names and classes you ask us to watch. We need these to check new U.S. trademark filings against your brand and send you alerts.
  • What you enter in the free name check. We store the email and brand name you enter. The check itself runs on Cloudflare, and the email and brand name are sent to our inbox through Web3Forms.
  • Billing details. Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card details. We never see or store your full card number. We do receive basic billing information from Stripe, like your name, email, plan, and payment status.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to support, we keep that conversation so we can help you.
  • Basic site analytics. We use GoatCounter to count page views and button clicks. It does not use tracking cookies and does not build a profile of you.

How we use it

  • To send your daily trademark alerts and run your subscription.
  • To send our free weekly trademark tips and a short welcome email series if you signed up for them. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link, and you can also reply or email us to stop them.
  • To answer support questions and send you important account emails, like trial and billing reminders.
  • To understand which pages are useful, using the GoatCounter totals described above.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We do not use ad trackers or advertising pixels on our site.

Services we use

We rely on a few companies to run ClashOwl. They only get the information they need to do their job for us.

  • Stripe for payments and billing.
  • Resend to send and receive our emails and keep our email list.
  • Web3Forms to deliver the signup and name check forms on our site to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare to run the free name check.
  • GitHub Pages to host this website.
  • GoatCounter for privacy friendly page view counts.
  • Google Fonts to load the fonts on this site, which means your browser requests font files from Google.

Like most websites, these services may see your IP address and browser details when your browser connects to them.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and watched brands while you are a customer. If you cancel, or ask us to delete your data, we will delete it, except billing records we need to keep for taxes and accounting. If you unsubscribe from our tips, we keep your email on a do not email list so we don't contact you again by mistake. Name check entries and newsletter signups are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Just email support@clashowl.com from the address you used with us. We will reply within 45 days.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it, to ask us to delete or correct it, and to not be treated differently for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in that law. To make a request, email support@clashowl.com. We may ask you to confirm your request from the same email address so we know it's really you.

Children

ClashOwl is a service for businesses and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
