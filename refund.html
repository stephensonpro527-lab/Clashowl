Refund Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

We want ClashOwl to be easy to try and easy to leave.

Free trial

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. You are not charged during the trial, so if ClashOwl is not for you, cancel before day 7 and you pay nothing.

Canceling

You can cancel anytime through the Stripe billing link in your emails or by emailing support@clashowl.com. Your alerts keep running until the end of the period you paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Monthly plan

Monthly payments are final and are not refunded, including for partial months. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Yearly plan

If you change your mind after a yearly charge, email us within 7 days of that charge and we will refund it in full and cancel your subscription. After 7 days, yearly payments are final and are not refunded for unused months, and you keep access until the year ends.

Billing mistakes

If you were charged twice, charged after canceling or charged the wrong amount, email us and we will refund the error in full.

How refunds work

Approved refunds go back to the original card through Stripe. They usually show up within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Contact

Email support@clashowl.com with the email address on your account. We reply within 2 business days.
