FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the opposition window?

After the USPTO approves an application, it’s published in the Official Gazette. Anyone who believes they’d be harmed has 30 days from publication to file an opposition or request more time. Hearing about a filing early gives you time to speak with an attorney before that window closes.

Is ClashOwl legal advice?

No. ClashOwl is an automated alert service, not a law firm. We flag filings that might conflict with your brand; whether there’s a real conflict, and what to do about it, are questions for a licensed trademark attorney.

Where does the data come from?

From the official daily trademark application data published by the U.S. Patent and Trademark Office. ClashOwl is not affiliated with the USPTO.

Do I need a registered trademark?

No. Anyone using a brand name can watch it, registered or not.

Will I get a lot of false alarms?

We rank matches by how closely they look and sound like your name and whether they’re in a related category, and we only email when there’s something worth a look. Each filing is reported once.
