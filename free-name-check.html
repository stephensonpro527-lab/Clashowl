What you'll see

  • How many U.S. trademark applications from roughly the last 60 days look or sound like your name, and why each one is similar.
  • When each was filed, its class (what kind of goods it covers), and its status.
  • The name, serial number, and USPTO record link for each match.
  • Nothing found? That only means no similar names in new filings from roughly the last 60 days. Older applications and registered marks are not checked, so use the “Search the full USPTO register” button too. ClashOwl can watch new filings for you going forward.

Disclaimer: this free check covers roughly the last 60 days of USPTO applications. It is not a full register report, not a clearance search, and not legal advice.
